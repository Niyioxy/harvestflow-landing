Church membership software that notices when someone is missing

A membership list tells you who joined. HarvestFlow also tells you who came on Sunday, who has drifted, and who is meant to be caring for them, so nobody slips away quietly.

Every member in one place

  • A searchable member directory. Phone, email, address, birthday, marital status, occupation and the date each person joined.
  • Membership stages. New member, full member, inactive or relocated, so you can filter the list and see who is settled and who is drifting.
  • Families. Link children to a parent. Children are counted at every service but never messaged; anything for them goes to the parent.
  • A carer for each member. Give each person an undershepherd, a cell group and a department, so everyone knows whose care they are in.
  • Bring your list with you. Import members from a CSV file and start taking registers the same day.

A register for every service

Many churches meet more than once a week: Sunday services, a midweek Bible study, a prayer meeting, a youth night. HarvestFlow keeps one register for each, for as many services as you hold.

Members check in

Print your church's QR poster. Members scan it with their phone camera as they arrive, with no app to install.

Leaders fill the gaps

Your leaders mark anyone else on their phones. If anyone records a person as present, they count as present.

The register is saved

Each service is recorded once, under its own name, so you can compare Sunday with midweek over time.

When a member is missing, someone notices

Most people do not leave a church in one day. They miss a Sunday, then another, and nobody calls. HarvestFlow turns each absence into care:

  • A "we missed you" message. After each service, members who weren't there can get a warm WhatsApp message from the church, at the time you choose for that service.
  • A pastoral care task. Each absent adult member becomes a task for their undershepherd, cell leader or department head, in that order. It closes itself when the person comes back.
  • An evening check for the admin. If a register wasn't taken or a care task has sat open for over a week, the church admin gets a short email that evening.

From new convert to member

Most membership software starts when someone joins. HarvestFlow starts at the altar call. New converts and first-time visitors are followed up first, and when someone settles in, they are promoted to a member in one tap with their details and history. Frequently asked questions

Can I import our current member list?

Yes. Upload a CSV file exported from a spreadsheet or your old system, and your members are added in one go.

Do members need to sign in?

No. Members never need an account. Your leaders sign in and keep the records, and members can check themselves in at services by scanning a QR code with their phone camera.

Who can see member details?

Each church has its own private workspace. Pastors, admins and coordinators manage members; cell leaders, undershepherds and department heads see the people in their care.

Does HarvestFlow track giving or tithes?

No. HarvestFlow focuses on people: follow-up, attendance, discipleship and care. It does not record giving, so you can keep using the giving system you already have.
