Why the first 48 hours matter

Most guides on visitor and convert follow-up say the same thing: reach people within a day or two, while the decision is fresh. After that, every week of silence makes a call harder and a return less likely.

The trouble is that follow-up usually depends on one or two busy people with a list. Some converts get called three times and others never. When someone asks how follow-up went, nobody can say. A shared follow-up system fixes this because the list, the owner and the result of every call are all in one place. A simple follow-up plan you can run with HarvestFlow

Many churches use a pattern like this. HarvestFlow gives you the records and reports to keep to it.

Day 0: register the convert

Capture name, phone, location and decision type at the outreach, by typing, by voice or by CSV import.

Within 48 hours: first call

The assigned worker taps the number, calls, and logs the outcome. "Contacted within 48 hours" is also the first discipleship milestone, so you can see who has been reached in time.

Week 1 to 2: bring them to church

Log whether they attended, and send a personalised WhatsApp invitation to those who did not answer.

Week 2 to 4: connect to a cell group

Record when they join a cell group and who looks after them.

Month 1, 3 and 6: check retention

Mark each person active, irregular or unreachable, and compare results across outreaches.

What your team can do

  • Work from an assigned list. Each worker sees only the converts given to them, so nobody is called twice or skipped.
  • Call in one tap and log the outcome. Responded, no answer, met in person and more, with notes. Follow-ups can also be logged as WhatsApp, visit or SMS.
  • See who needs contact. The follow-up page lists converts who have not been reached and shows how each team member is doing.
  • Send a personalised message. Write once, use [Name] and every convert receives a message with their own name.
  • Keep working offline. View converts and log calls without a connection. Everything syncs when the phone is back online.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly should we follow up a new convert?

Most church follow-up guides recommend contact within 24 to 48 hours of the decision. HarvestFlow tracks "contacted within 48 hours" as the first discipleship milestone for every convert.

Who sees which converts?

Workers see only the converts assigned to them. Coordinators manage their team, and admins see everyone in the church.

Can we follow up by WhatsApp as well as by phone call?

Yes. Follow-ups can be logged as a call, WhatsApp, visit or SMS, and you can send personalised WhatsApp messages to your converts from the app.

Can we use it with our existing spreadsheet of converts?

Yes. Upload it with bulk CSV import and then assign the converts to workers.
