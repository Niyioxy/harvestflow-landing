Church first-time visitor follow-up, without anyone being missed

A visitor who is welcomed warmly and contacted soon afterwards is far more likely to return. HarvestFlow captures first-timers at the door, sends the welcome for you, and gives a welcomer a call to make.

Capture first-timers in seconds

Visitors rarely fill in long forms. HarvestFlow gives you two quick ways to record them:

  • A QR check-in. The visitor scans a code, chooses "First time here?" and adds their name and number on their own phone.
  • A welcome card. An usher or leader types the details from a card into the app. Workers, cell leaders and other team members can do this, so you are not relying on one person.

The visitor says whether they are happy to receive messages, and the app respects that. You can also stop messages to anyone who asks.

A welcome sequence that sends itself

For visitors who have agreed to hear from you, HarvestFlow sends:

A welcome, straight away

A friendly message in your church name thanking them for coming.

A midweek note, three days later

A short, personal check-in that asks if there is anything you can pray with them about.

An invitation the evening before the next service

It goes out on the day before your next in-person service, with the service name and time.

You can edit all three messages and choose the time of day they are sent. Messages use [Name] and your church name, so each one is addressed personally.

A person, not just a message

Every new visitor is given a welcomer, a real person on your team who is asked to call them. The welcomer sees who they are following up and records the outcome, so the call does not depend on someone remembering. For what the welcomer and the rest of the team do each day of the first week, read how to follow up first-time visitors at church: a simple 7-day plan.

If a visitor comes again, HarvestFlow recognises their phone number as a return visit and updates their record, so you can see who has come once and who is coming regularly.

After the service

When you run events that people book in advance, HarvestFlow can also send a reminder on the day, a thank-you the next day to first-timers who came, and a "we missed you" note to those who booked but did not. Read more about the messaging side on our church WhatsApp follow-up page.

Frequently asked questions

Do visitors need to install anything?

No. The QR check-in opens in the phone browser, and nothing needs to be downloaded.

Do we need the visitor's permission to message them?

Yes, and HarvestFlow asks for it. Messages are sent only to visitors who have agreed, and you can stop messages to anyone who asks. Your church is responsible for following the rules that apply where your visitors live.

Can we change the welcome messages?

Yes. The welcome, the midweek note and the invitation are all editable, and you choose the time they go out.

How does it know when someone returns?

It matches the phone number. A number it already knows is treated as a return visit instead of a new first-timer.

Does it work for online services too?

Visitors can be recorded for any service. The invitation message is timed to your next in-person service.
