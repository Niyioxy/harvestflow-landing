When paper isn't enough

These printables work well for a handful of people. When your church is following up dozens, HarvestFlow gives each person a named worker, records every call, shows who hasn't been reached, and sends welcome and "we missed you" messages for you. It can even read a photo of filled-in cards or a paper register. It's free for churches during the beta. Read our guides on the blog, starting with what to say on your first call to a new convert.
