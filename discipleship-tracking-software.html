Five milestones for every convert

Each convert's profile tracks the steps that matter most in the first months:

Contacted within 48 hours

Someone from your team has reached them while the decision is fresh.

First church attendance

They have come to a service.

Joined a cell group

They belong to a small group and have someone who knows them.

Baptised

They have taken that public step.

Joined a ministry unit

They are serving, not just attending.

Open any profile and you see the journey at a glance. Open the list and you see who is stuck on which step.

Retention you can measure

Retention is the honest test of discipleship, and it is usually the number a church does not have. HarvestFlow records each convert's status at month 1, month 3 and month 6: active, irregular, not reachable, backslid, transferred or moved away. The retention dashboard then shows percentages by outreach or by decision type, so you can learn which outreaches produce lasting disciples.

Frequently asked questions

What does HarvestFlow track for discipleship?

Five milestones (contacted within 48 hours, first church attendance, joined a cell group, baptised and joined a ministry unit) plus retention status at month 1, month 3 and month 6.

What statuses can a convert have at each retention check?

Active, irregular, not reachable, backslid, transferred or moved away.

Can we see retention for a specific outreach?

Yes. The retention page shows percentages by crusade or outreach, and by decision type.

Can I export the data?

Yes. The registry, follow-up report and retention summary can all be downloaded as CSV.

Is this only for new converts?

Milestones and retention are designed around converts. First-time visitors and members are tracked in their own follow-up and attendance tools.
