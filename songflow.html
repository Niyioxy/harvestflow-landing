What Songflow does

Listens for the song

It matches the opening words your leader sings against your song library and brings up the lyrics, then follows the verses and choruses as they are sung.

Projector and big screen

A separate display window shows the current verse, large and clear. It fills a second monitor or projector when one is connected.

Congregation phones

Members scan a QR code on the same WiFi and follow the lyrics in their phone's browser. Nothing to install.

Bible on screen

Search by reference or by voice. Seven translations are built in: KJV, ASV, BSB, Darby, NHEB, Webster and YLT.

Setlists

Plan the service ahead and Songflow favours those songs when it listens.

Your own songs

Add songs by hand or import them from text, Word and PDF files. 20 public domain hymns are included to start.

Voice matching is still being tuned, so treat it as a helper rather than a replacement for a person. You can always pick a song by hand and move between verses with the Next and Prev buttons. For your first services, have someone ready at the keyboard.

Set up in four steps

Install it. Download the installer above and run it. Songflow installs for your Windows user and adds a desktop shortcut.

Add your Deepgram key. Songflow uses Deepgram for speech recognition, and you use your own account. Create a free account at console.deepgram.com, make an API key, then choose Voice key at the top of Songflow and paste it in. It is stored encrypted on your computer.

Point it at your screen. With a second monitor or projector connected, the display window opens full screen on it; otherwise it opens in a window you can drag there. Scan the QR code in the right-hand panel with a phone on the same WiFi to check the congregation view.

Start listening. Choose your microphone, ideally a feed from the sound desk rather than the laptop's built-in mic, then press Start Listening.

Deepgram charges for the audio it transcribes, and new accounts may include free credit. Check deepgram.com/pricing for the current terms before you plan a regular schedule.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Songflow's installer is not yet code-signed, and Windows shows this warning for any new, unsigned program. It does not mean the file is infected.

To continue, select More info, then Run anyway.

If you want to be sure you have the file we published, compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the one in the download box above. In PowerShell, from the folder you saved it in:

Get-FileHash .\Songflow-Setup-1.0.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256

The two values must match exactly. If they do not, delete the file and download it again from this page.

What leaves your computer

  • While you are listening, microphone audio is sent to Deepgram under your own account, and the text comes back. That is the only internet connection Songflow makes.
  • Your songs, setlists and settings stay on your computer. HarvestFlow does not receive them.
  • Songflow keeps a small diagnostics log on your computer: timings, match scores and the microphone name. It does not save what was said. Old logs are deleted after 30 days.
  • There is no account, no tracking and no automatic update. To update, download the next version from this page.

Phones connect to Songflow directly over your church's WiFi. Anyone on that network who has the address can view the lyrics, so use your church's own network rather than a public one.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. There is no Mac or Linux version yet.
  • A microphone or a line feed from your sound desk.
  • An internet connection for voice listening, and a Deepgram API key.
  • A second monitor or projector for the big screen, and WiFi for congregation phones.

Frequently asked questions

Is Songflow free?

The app is free to download and use. Voice listening runs through Deepgram, a separate speech service you sign up to yourself and pay for according to your use.

Does it work on Mac or phones?

Not yet. Songflow is a Windows 10 or 11 desktop app. Congregation members follow along in the browser on any phone, with nothing to install.

Does it include worship songs?

It includes 20 public domain hymns. Contemporary songs are not included: add them yourself or import them from text, Word or PDF files, under your church's own licence.

Do I need internet?

Voice listening needs internet because the audio goes to Deepgram. Choosing songs by hand, the Bible, the projector display and phone sync use only your own computer and WiFi.

Where are my songs stored?

On your computer, in the songflow folder inside your Windows AppData Roaming folder. Back up songflow.json from that folder to keep your library.

How do I remove it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find Songflow and choose Uninstall.
