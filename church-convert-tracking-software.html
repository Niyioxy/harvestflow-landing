Why churches lose converts after the altar call

The decision is the easy part. The weeks after it are where people slip away. Decision cards end up in a drawer. Workers say they called, but nobody can check. A year later, no one can say how many people from last year's crusade are still in church.

Convert tracking software closes those gaps. Every person has a record, every record has an owner, and every call leaves a trace, so leadership can see who has been reached and who has been missed. For more on why this happens, read why most churches lose new converts in the first 30 days.

How convert tracking works in HarvestFlow

Register the convert

Capture name, phone, gender, age group, location and decision type at the outreach. Speak the details with voice registration, add them by hand, or import a spreadsheet in bulk.

Assign a worker and call

Each follow-up worker sees only the converts assigned to them. They tap a number to call, then log the outcome: responded, no answer, met in person and more.

Track the milestones

Follow each person through five steps: contacted within 48 hours, first church attendance, joined a cell group, baptised and joined a ministry unit.

Measure retention

Record whether each convert is active, irregular or unreachable at month 1, month 3 and month 6, and compare results by crusade or decision type.

Frequently asked questions

What is church convert tracking software?

It is software that records everyone who makes a decision for Christ at an outreach, crusade or service, and tracks what happens next: who called them, whether they came to church, joined a cell group, were baptised and became active. It replaces paper decision cards and scattered spreadsheets.

How is it different from a church management system?

A general church management system focuses on members, giving and events. HarvestFlow focuses on the moment of decision and the weeks after it, when most converts are lost. It gives your follow-up team assigned lists, call logging and retention reporting.

Do my workers need to install an app?

No. HarvestFlow runs in a phone browser. Workers sign in, see the converts assigned to them and tap a number to call.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Workers can view their converts and log calls offline, and everything syncs when the phone is back online.

Can I import my existing list of converts?

Yes. HarvestFlow supports bulk CSV import, so you can upload the spreadsheet you already have.

How much does it cost?

HarvestFlow is free for churches during the beta. Registered churches will be told before any charge begins.

Is our convert data private?

Each church has its own workspace, and workers only see the converts their role allows. Our privacy policy lists the service providers involved.
