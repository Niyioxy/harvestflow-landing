HarvestFlow is free for churches during the beta

Every feature, every worker and leader, and messaging included. Free access is open to the first 50 churches.

What's included

Convert tracking

Register every decision, including by voice, and assign each person to a worker.

Follow-up

One-tap calls, recorded outcomes, milestones and next steps.

First-time visitors

QR check-in and a welcome sequence for visitors who agree to it.

Members and attendance

Cell groups, departments, registers and pastoral care.

WhatsApp and SMS

Personalised and scheduled messages, sent through HarvestFlow.

Everything else

Reports and CSV export, offline use, roles and privacy, the ministers' rota and Barnabas, the in-app guide.

After the beta

Pricing will be announced before the beta ends, and every registered church will be told before any charge begins. Churches that join during the beta will keep generous pricing as a thank-you for helping shape HarvestFlow.

One thing to know about messaging: WhatsApp and SMS coverage depends on the messaging provider and varies by country (for example, SMS does not currently reach UK numbers). Test with a few numbers when you start.

Frequently asked questions

Is HarvestFlow really free?

Yes. During the beta there is no charge to register a church, add workers and leaders, or use any feature, including WhatsApp and SMS messages sent through HarvestFlow.

What happens when the beta ends?

Pricing will be announced before the beta ends, and every registered church will be told before any charge begins. Churches that join during the beta will keep generous pricing.

Do I need a card to register?

No. Register with your church's details and you receive a login link by email.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. You can download your registry, follow-up history, attendance and retention as CSV files at any time.
