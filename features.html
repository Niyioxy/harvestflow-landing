Frequently asked questions

What is HarvestFlow?

HarvestFlow is a web platform that helps churches follow up new converts and first-time visitors, track discipleship, take attendance in cells and send personalised messages.

Who is it for?

It is built for Pentecostal and Charismatic churches that run regular outreaches, but any church that wants to follow people up well can use it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. HarvestFlow runs in a phone or computer browser.

How much does it cost?

It is free for churches during the beta.
