Everything HarvestFlow does for church follow-up

One platform follows each person from the first decision or first visit to a settled place in your church. Start with the area that matters most to you.

Register your church free Set up in under 24 hours. Works on any phone with a browser.

Follow-up and discipleship

Convert tracking

Register every decision, assign workers and see who has been reached.

New convert follow-up

A shared list, one-tap calls and a record of every conversation.

First-time visitors

QR check-in, an automatic welcome sequence and a welcomer to call.

Cell groups and attendance

Leader registers, absence follow-up and pastoral care tasks.

WhatsApp and SMS messaging

Personalised, scheduled and weekly messages with a fallback.

Discipleship tracking

Five milestones and retention at month 1, 3 and 6.

Membership and care

When a convert settles, they become a member, and HarvestFlow keeps caring for them. Read more about our church membership software.

Member directory

Phone, email, address, birthday, marital status, occupation and joining date for every member, all searchable.

Membership stages

New member, full member, inactive or relocated, so you can see who is settled and who is drifting.

Convert to member

Promote a settled convert to a member in one tap. Their details come with them.

Families and children

Link children to a parent. Children are counted at services but never messaged; anything for them goes to the parent.

Attendance registers

One register for each service, however many you hold in a week, plus QR self check-in at the door.

"We missed you" messages

Members who missed a service get a caring WhatsApp message from the church at the time you choose.

Pastoral care tasks

Each absent adult member becomes a task for their undershepherd or cell leader, and closes itself when they return.

Cell groups and departments

Cell leaders and department heads see their own people and take their own registers.

Import your member list

Bring members in from a CSV spreadsheet and start taking registers the same day.

Across the whole platform

Barnabas, your guide

A built-in assistant that answers questions about the app and about the records you are allowed to see. It can also speak and listen.

Voice registration

Speak a convert's details instead of typing them, in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Afrikaans, Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa and Pidgin.

Works offline

View people and log calls with no connection. Everything syncs when you are back online.

Roles and privacy

Each church has its own workspace, and every role sees only what it should.

Ministers' rota

A fair monthly rota for the ministers your church chooses, drafted for you to review and publish.

Reports and CSV export

Download your registry, follow-up history, attendance and retention at any time.

Frequently asked questions

What is HarvestFlow?

HarvestFlow is a web platform that helps churches follow up new converts and first-time visitors, track discipleship, keep a member directory, take attendance at every service and in cells, and send personalised messages.

Who is it for?

It is built for Pentecostal and Charismatic churches that run regular outreaches, but any church that wants to follow people up well can use it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. HarvestFlow runs in a phone or computer browser.

How much does it cost?

It is free for churches during the beta.
