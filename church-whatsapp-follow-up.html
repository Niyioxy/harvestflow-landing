Church WhatsApp follow-up that reaches people by name

Most of your people already live on WhatsApp. HarvestFlow lets you write a message once and send it to everyone by name, now or on a schedule, without copying and pasting a single number.

Register your church free Set up in under 24 hours. Works on any phone with a browser.

What you can send

  • One-click updates. Write the message, use [Name] and your church name, and send it to your converts or your members.
  • WhatsApp first, SMS as a fallback. If a message cannot go by WhatsApp, HarvestFlow can try SMS where your provider supports the destination country. You choose whether the fallback is on.
  • Scheduled sends. Pick a date and time and the message goes out even when nobody has the app open.
  • Weekly repeating updates. Set a Sunday reminder or a midweek prayer note once, and it repeats on the days you choose.
  • A plan-ahead queue. Line up messages for the coming weeks, edit them, or cancel one before it goes.

Planning a month of messages

If writing every message is the hard part, the planner can draft them for you. Give it a theme, a scripture and any notes, choose the dates, and it suggests a different message for each date. You read each one, edit it, or write your own before anything is scheduled. Nothing is sent without your approval.

Messages that follow people through the journey

The same messaging works across your church:

  • A welcome, a midweek note and an invitation for first-time visitors.
  • A "we missed you" message after a service, timed for each service. See cell group management.
  • Reminders and thank-yous around events people book in advance.
  • Personalised updates to new converts you are following up.

Sending responsibly

Messages go out in your church's name, so make sure people have agreed to hear from you and are able to stop. HarvestFlow asks for consent from first-time visitors, and you can stop messages to anyone who opts out. Delivery uses a third-party messaging provider, and coverage varies by country, so test with a few numbers first. Our privacy policy explains who handles messages and numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Do the messages come from our own WhatsApp number?

Messages are delivered through HarvestFlow's messaging provider and linked WhatsApp number. Check the sender details with a few test messages before a large send.

Can we personalise each message?

Yes. Use [Name] and your church name, and each person receives a message addressed to them.

Can we schedule messages in advance?

Yes. You can send at a set date and time, repeat on chosen days each week, or queue messages for the coming weeks.

Does AI write the messages for us?

The planner can draft messages from your theme and notes, but you review and edit every one before it is scheduled.

Will SMS work in every country?

No. SMS and WhatsApp coverage depends on the provider and the destination, so test with a few numbers in each country you serve.
