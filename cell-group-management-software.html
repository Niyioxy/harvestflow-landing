Cell group management software that shows who is missing

A cell leader should not have to carry the whole flock in their head. HarvestFlow gives each leader a simple register, tells the right person when someone is absent, and keeps care visible to the pastor.

Attendance each leader can take on a phone

  • My Cell. Cell leaders see their own people and take attendance for cell meetings and for church services.
  • One record per person per service. Attendance is saved once, so a register that is edited later never doubles up.
  • My flock for undershepherds. Leaders who look after several cells see everyone under their care.
  • Department heads. A department head has a page for the people in their department.
  • Roles that match your structure. Pastors, general overseers, undershepherds, cell leaders and workers each see what their role allows.

When someone is absent, the right person finds out

Taking attendance is only useful if someone acts on it. HarvestFlow turns an absence into a follow-up:

The register is saved

The leader marks who came. Present wins if a person is recorded by more than one leader.

A "we missed you" message goes out

You choose the message, which services trigger it and a send time for each service.

A care task is created

The person's carer sees who to call, in the order your church sets: undershepherd, then cell leader, then unit head.

The pastor sees the result

The pastoral care page lists open care tasks and who each one is assigned to, so you can assign any that are still open.

From converts to cells

New converts are not just names in a list. Joining a cell group is one of the five discipleship milestones HarvestFlow tracks, so you can see which converts have been connected to a cell and which are still waiting. See discipleship tracking software for how milestones work.

Frequently asked questions

Can a cell leader use it on a phone?

Yes. HarvestFlow runs in a phone browser, with nothing to install, and it can be used offline for viewing people and logging calls.

What happens if two leaders mark the same person differently?

If the person is recorded as present by any of their carers, they are counted as present, so people are not chased by mistake.

Who gets the care task for an absent person?

The task goes to the person's carer, in the order your church uses: undershepherd, cell leader, then unit head.

Is department meeting attendance supported?

Departments can see their people and church service attendance today. Separate registers for department meetings are still being built.
